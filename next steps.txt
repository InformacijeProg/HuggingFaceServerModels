Marx, Kapital, book one (Zagreb 1947, 903 pages): the only free Croatian edition is an OCR scan that had lost its diacritics. I added a diacritics restorer that learns a lexicon from the other books plus 20,000 Croatian Wikipedia articles and restored 32,000 words. The text still has letter-level OCR errors and 1947 vocabulary with Serbian traces, so use it as a small share of the corpus, not its core.
What I rejected and why. The Serbo-Croatian Marx texts on marxists.org are the Belgrade translation in Serbian (ekavian vrednost, reč), which would teach the model Serbian forms. Meditations and Goldman's essays are downloaded as Croatian only, because their English editions align too poorly. Several BEK titles turned out to be poetry or unavailable and were swapped out.
